CHORDSTAGE Germanikkus · Quick guide

Play live on the MPK mini Plus II (or the PopuPiano) without hitting a wrong chord: one key per chord, a left hand that stays in key, and a screen that shows what comes next. Colors match pages 2–4; page 5 covers the playing tools.

The workflow

1Set the key

Pick the song's Key and Scale, then a Sound. Every note you play is corrected to that key.

Top left

2Set the zones

Bass keys: press your top left-hand key. Split at: where chords end and melody starts.

⚙ Settings › Keys setup

3Progression

Type it in: Imaj7 vi7 ii7 V7 or G D Em C. Add [Verse] / [Chorus] labels.

Progression box

4Save the song

+ Save as new song keeps the key, sound, zones and progression. Build your set list.

Song bar · ⚙ Settings › Set list

5Play

Left hand: bass notes. Middle: one key = one full chord, or Chord lock to play real shapes. Right: melody.

MPK keys + pads

6Follow along

The next chord lights up. Play it and the strip moves on. Or just tap pad 8 for the next chord.

Strip + chord tiles

7MPC sound

Optional: send the corrected notes to MPC 3 and play its instruments instead of the built-in sounds.

⚙ Settings › Sound & MIDI out

8Perform

⏺ Rec a loop and solo over it. Next song: ▶, arrows, a pedal or knob 8. Esc stops every note.

On stage

From Chord Stage to MPC 3

Once on the Mac

  1. Open Audio MIDI Setup (Applications → Utilities).
  2. Window → Show MIDI Studio, double-click IAC Driver.
  3. Check Device is online.
➜

In Chord Stage

Send MIDI to: IAC Driver Bus 1 → MPC
Channel 1 · chords, melody, left hand
Channel 2 · bass guitar (if chosen)

Uncheck Built-in sound so you only hear MPC.

➜

In MPC 3

  1. Load a keys or keygroup program on a track.
  2. Set that track's MIDI input to IAC Driver, not the MPK. Otherwise you hear the uncorrected notes too.
  3. Bass guitar: a second track listening on channel 2.

Playing tools

Smooth voicing

On by default. Each chord uses the inversion closest to the last one, so changes glide instead of jumping around the keyboard, the way neo soul and gospel players move. The bass sits low underneath. Untick it for plain root-position chords; MPC chords keep their own voicings.

Strum

Rolls each chord from the bottom note up, like a classical pianist: Light, Medium, Slow roll or Harp. Let go early and the rest of the roll is skipped. Saved with each song.

Arp

Plays every chord one note at a time in time with the Tempo, from any keyboard: Up, Down, Up & down or Random; 1/8, 1/8 triplet or 1/16; 1 or 2 octaves. A low root holds as the bass. Arp wins over Strum. The MPK’s own ARP button works too, at the MPK’s tempo.

Song form

Under the progression. Plays it as a song: 3×, then down a step, lift a half step (twice), up a 4th, a bridge, or 2×, bridge, down a step, home. Custom…: e.g. Main x3, Main -2, Bridge, Main +1 (x3 repeats, +2/−2 moves half steps, Bridge uses your [Bridge] or writes one). The bar shows the part and pass you’re on.

Record & loop

⏺ Rec (R) records what Chord Stage plays. With the beat on it starts on bar 1 and keeps whole bars; without it, it starts on your first note. A waiting Rec blinks yellow and cancels itself after 20 s with no notes. Press it again and the loop plays (70% volume) so you can solo. ▶ Loop (L) starts/stops, ✕ Clear erases, ⬇ MIDI file saves it for the MPC. On the MPK: Record, Loop, Undo (clear) and ⊕ (Melody over).

Melody over

🎵 Melody over (M) turns the whole keyboard into a solo instrument while the loop plays: every key plays one note in the key, an octave up, no chords. Up next shows a reminder while it’s on, and Clear or a new Rec turns it off.

Stage view

Just what you need while playing: song, big chord and Up next, progression and song form, chord tiles, Beat, Rec / Loop and STOP ALL. Phones get it automatically; on a tablet or Mac press ▣ Stage view. ⚙ All controls brings everything back.

On the Android tablet

Open germanikkus-music.github.io/chord-stage in Chrome, then ⋮ → Install app. It gets its own icon and works offline. Connect the MPK by USB, or the PopuPiano over Bluetooth with MIDI BLE Connect (disconnect it from the MPC first). Use the aux cable: a Bluetooth speaker adds about 0.2 s of delay. The PopuPiano’s six left bars play bass notes 1–6 of the song’s key.

Horn mode (Aerophone)

Pick Horn at the start, or with the 🎹/🎺 button. Horn sound: Recorder, Flute, Clarinet, Trumpet, Violin, Saw lead, Wind synth, Wind pad or Same as Sound; breath sets the volume (and brightness on Trumpet, Saw lead and Wind synth). Chords: the next chord’s root moves the progression (silent or playing), One note = one chord (fingerings C to B play the key’s chords I–vii), or Melody only. Tune fixes a shifted transpose. The keyboard area shows the fingerings.

Beat

▶ Beat or Space starts and stops it. Pick a style (the number is its usual tempo), type a Tempo, press Tap, or use the MPK’s Tap Tempo or encoder. Start on first chord: the drums come in when you play. Drums to MPC: GM drum notes on channel 10. Each song saves its beat.

Chord lock vs Sloppy mode

Chord lock (a Lower/Upper keys choice): put your whole hand down; every key is silent except the next chord’s root, which plays the full chord. Lift and play the next shape. Sloppy mode: other keys still play their own chords.

Writing progressions

Numbers follow the key. Upper case = major, lower case = minor; add 7, maj7, ° or b (bVII). Red chips are typos. ⚙ Settings › Set list: + MPC files or + Add folder imports .progression and .mpcpattern files with their voicings; a green box marks the next key.

Keep a backup

Songs live in each browser separately (Mac Chrome, the tablet). Press ⚙ Settings › Set list → Export backup; Import brings it back or moves it to another device.
